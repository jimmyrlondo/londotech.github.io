Don’t let the deadline pass

Too often, people call us about an injury claim only to learn the statute of limitations has already expired. The statute of limitations is the deadline to formally file a lawsuit, and in Ohio it depends on the type of claim. For most injury claims it is generally two years, and for medical malpractice it can be as short as one year.

Calling early gives us time to examine your situation and collect the evidence your claim needs, and to file a lawsuit on time if one is necessary. We can also help stop collection calls from medical providers.

What compensation can cover

Compensation varies a great deal depending on your situation. Beyond your medical bills, we can help you seek compensation for non-economic damages such as pain and suffering, and punitive damages when harm was caused by intentional misconduct or gross negligence.

  • Past and future medical bills
  • Lost wages
  • Pain and suffering
