About the firm

Attorney Scott “Storey” Smith

Scott “Storey” Smith has practiced law in Ohio since 1995. For his zealous representation, he is recognized by The National Trial Lawyers: Top 100, an invitation-only organization of trial lawyers from each state.

Attorney Smith and his legal team are committed to giving every client sound legal advice and aggressive representation, and to pursuing the best possible outcome in every case. An attorney for the people, he serves clients throughout Ohio in criminal law, family law and personal injury.

Attorney Smith’s family has been part of the greater Cleveland community since 1962, when his grandfather, Charles Edward Stourton Chapman, came here to launch Cuyahoga Community College as its founding president. The firm displays the Stourton family crest in honor of his lifelong devotion to higher education and to the people of Northeast Ohio.
