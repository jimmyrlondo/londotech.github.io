The attorney

An attorney for the people.

Scott “Storey” Smith has practiced law in Ohio since 1995, in courtrooms from Elyria to Cleveland and across the state.

He and his legal team are committed to giving every client sound legal advice and aggressive representation, and to pursuing the best possible outcome in every case. He serves clients throughout Ohio in criminal law, family law and personal injury, and has tried numerous jury trials at the state and municipal level.

The crest

Rooted in Northeast Ohio since 1962

Attorney Smith’s grandfather, Charles Edward Stourton Chapman, came to Cleveland in 1962 to launch Cuyahoga Community College as its founding president. The firm displays the Stourton family crest in honor of his lifelong devotion to higher education and to the people of Northeast Ohio.
