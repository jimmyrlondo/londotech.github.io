Frequently asked questions

Please reach us at hello@recoupandreliefcounseling.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

What is the cost of a counseling session?

Session rates vary based on the therapist and service type. Please contact our office and we'll be happy to walk you through pricing.

How long is a typical counseling session?

Most counseling sessions last about 50 minutes, though some may be slightly longer or shorter depending on your needs and treatment plan.

What issues can counseling help with?

Our therapists support clients with a wide range of concerns, including anxiety, depression, relationship challenges, grief and loss, trauma, stress, life transitions, and more.

Do you offer marriage counseling in Medina?

Yes. Our Medina office provides marriage and couples counseling to help partners strengthen communication, rebuild trust, and grow together.

Can I find anxiety therapy in Medina?

Absolutely. We have licensed therapists in Medina who specialize in treating anxiety and helping clients build healthy coping strategies.

Do you provide grief counseling in Medina?

Yes. We offer compassionate grief counseling to support individuals and families as they navigate loss and healing.

How do I choose the right therapist in Medina?

You can view our Meet the Therapists page or call our office. We'll guide you to a counselor whose expertise and style are the best fit for your needs.

Is counseling in Medina covered by insurance?

We accept many major insurance providers including Medicare, Medicaid, Anthem, Aetna, and more. Contact us to confirm your plan.

Do you offer both in-person and teletherapy sessions in Medina?

Yes. You can choose to meet with your therapist in our Medina office or through secure online teletherapy sessions.
