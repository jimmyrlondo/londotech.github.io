Telehealth

Online therapy, anywhere in Ohio

Meet with a licensed therapist through secure, HIPAA-compliant video sessions from home, work, or wherever you feel comfortable. 13 of our telehealth therapists are accepting new clients.

Request an appointment See telehealth therapists

Why clients choose teletherapy

Teletherapy makes it easier to keep showing up for yourself. Bad weather, a cold going around, or a packed schedule doesn't have to mean a missed session.

You can still attend therapy before work, on your lunch break, or after the day is done, without adding a commute.

How it works

  • Secure, HIPAA-compliant video sessions
  • Available to clients anywhere in Ohio
  • The same therapists who see clients in our Medina and Dublin offices
  • Switch between online and in-office sessions when your therapist offers both
  • 17 insurance plans accepted, plus private pay
