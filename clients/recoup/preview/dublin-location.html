Why choose Recoup + Relief Counseling in Dublin?

Our goal is to create a space where you feel comfortable, understood, and empowered to make meaningful changes. We know that reaching out for help isn't always easy, which is why we strive to make the process as simple and supportive as possible from your very first appointment.

Frequently asked questions

Please reach us at hello@recoupandreliefcounseling.com if you cannot find an answer to your question.

How long will it take to see results from counseling?

Everyone's experience is different. Some clients notice improvements within a few sessions, while others benefit from longer-term support depending on their goals and the challenges they're working through.

What qualifications do your therapists have?

Our therapists are licensed professionals with advanced training in counseling or related fields. They have experience supporting individuals, couples, and families through a wide range of concerns.

What age groups do you work with?

We provide counseling for individuals of all ages, including children, teens, and adults.

Do you provide anxiety counseling in Dublin, Ohio?

Yes. Our Dublin office offers therapy for individuals experiencing anxiety, with approaches tailored to your specific needs.

Can I get depression counseling in Dublin?

Absolutely. We provide supportive, evidence-based counseling for depression at our Dublin office.

What types of therapy are available at your Dublin location?

We offer individual, couples, and family counseling in Dublin, as well as specialized therapy for anxiety, depression, and life transitions.

Do you offer teletherapy for Dublin clients?

Yes. We provide both in-person counseling and secure online teletherapy to fit your lifestyle.

Does insurance cover therapy in Dublin?

We accept many major insurance plans and also provide private pay options. Please contact us to check your coverage.

How do I schedule an appointment in Dublin?
