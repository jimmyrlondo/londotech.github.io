Meet Rebekkah: Trauma-Focused Therapy in Dublin, Ohio

July 26, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist isn’t just about working through the past. It’s about building a healthier, more connected relationship with yourself and others moving forward. At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Rebekkah provides therapy in Dublin, Ohio and via telehealth for teens and adults navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, and relationship challenges. Her approach is trauma-informed, empowering, and rooted in the belief that every person has the capacity for growth and healing.

Healing from Trauma and Moving Forward

Many people come to therapy carrying experiences that continue to impact how they think, feel, and relate to others.

You might be:

● Processing past trauma or PTSD

● Struggling with anxiety, depression, or mood changes

● Noticing patterns in relationships that feel difficult to change

● Feeling disconnected from yourself or others

● Navigating life transitions or emotional challenges

Rebekkah helps clients explore these experiences in a way that feels safe, supportive, and focused on growth.

Together, you’ll begin to:

● Understand how past experiences are shaping the present

● Identify patterns in thoughts, emotions, and relationships

● Build tools for emotional regulation and coping

● Move toward healthier, more fulfilling connections

A Trauma-Informed, Growth-Focused Approach

Rebekkah’s work is grounded in a trauma-focused lens, combined with a deep belief in each client’s ability to grow and change.

She integrates a range of evidence-based approaches, including:

● Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR)

● Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT)

● Parts work and internal systems approaches

These approaches allow clients to:

● Process and release difficult experiences

● Understand different parts of themselves

● Build greater emotional balance and self-awareness

● Create lasting change at a deeper level

Building Healthier Relationships With Yourself and Others

A central focus of Rebekkah’s work is helping clients improve their relationships.

This includes:

● Strengthening self-connection and self-understanding

● Improving communication and emotional awareness

● Navigating challenges in family or romantic relationships

● Breaking patterns that no longer serve you

She also works with families and couples, helping them build stronger, more supportive dynamics. Rebekkah works with teens and adults who are ready to explore their experiences and create meaningful change.

She is a strong fit for clients who are:

● Processing trauma or PTSD

● Experiencing anxiety, depression, or mood disorders

● Navigating relationship challenges or family dynamics

● Working through adjustment or life transitions

● Wanting to better understand themselves and their patterns

Therapy That Supports Growth and Connection

Rebekkah believes that healing is not just about reducing symptoms. It’s about creating a stronger, more connected sense of self.

Through therapy, clients can begin to:

● Feel more grounded and emotionally balanced

● Build healthier relationships

● Develop greater self-awareness and resilience

● Move forward with confidence and clarity

Start Therapy in Dublin, Ohio or via Telehealth

Rebekkah offers therapy in Dublin, Ohio and via telehealth for teens and adults seeking trauma- informed, supportive care. If you’re ready to begin healing and build stronger connections in your life, this may be a strong fit. Reach out today to schedule a consultation and see if working with Rebekkah feels right for you.
