Meet Shaquoi: Inclusive, Culturally Competent Therapy in Ohio

August 5, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist isn’t just about support. It’s about working with someone who sees you, respects your experiences, and creates a space where you can truly be yourself.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Shaquoi provides therapy both in-person(Medina office) and via telehealth, offering an inclusive, affirming, and collaborative approach for individuals navigating mental health challenges, identity exploration, and personal growth. Her work is grounded in empathy, cultural awareness, and a deep belief in each client’s ability to create meaningful change.

A Safe Space to Be Fully Yourself

Starting therapy can feel vulnerable, but it should never feel unsafe or judgmental.

You might be:

● Navigating anxiety or depression

● Processing trauma or difficult experiences

● Exploring identity, including gender or sexuality

● Struggling with self-esteem or confidence

● Feeling overwhelmed or unsure of your next step

Shaquoi creates a space where clients can show up exactly as they are; without pressure to filter, explain, or minimize their experiences.

Together, you’ll begin to:

● Explore your thoughts, emotions, and patterns

● Identify challenges and areas for growth

● Build tools to navigate life more effectively

● Move toward your personal goals with clarity and support

An Affirming and Culturally Competent Approach

Shaquoi is passionate about providing therapy that is both LGBTQIA+ affirming and culturally responsive.

She understands that identity, culture, and lived experiences play a significant role in mental health and that therapy should reflect that.

Her work is grounded in:

● Respect for each individual’s identity and background

● Awareness of cultural and systemic influences

● Creating a space that feels inclusive and validating

● Supporting clients in exploring and embracing who they are

This allows clients to feel not only supported, but truly understood.

Therapy as a Collaborative Process

Shaquoi believes that therapy works best when it is a partnership.

She approaches her work with the understanding that:

● You are the expert in your own life

● Therapy is a collaborative process, not a directive one

● Growth happens when clients feel empowered and involved

Her role is to support, guide, and walk alongside you as you work toward your goals.

Evidence-Based Support for Real Change

Shaquoi integrates evidence-based approaches to help clients build skills and create lasting change.

Her work includes:

● Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to understand and shift thought patterns

● Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) to build flexibility and values-driven action

These approaches allow clients to:

● Develop healthier ways of thinking

● Build coping skills for everyday challenges

● Strengthen emotional resilience

● Take meaningful steps toward the life they want

Who Shaquoi Works Best With

Shaquoi works with individuals across a wide range of backgrounds and experiences, including adolescents, young adults, and adults.

She is a strong fit for clients who are:

● Navigating anxiety, depression, or trauma

● Exploring identity, including gender and sexuality

● Seeking LGBTQIA+-affirming therapy

● Working through self-esteem or confidence challenges

● Looking for a collaborative, empowering therapy experience

● Couples seeking support with communication, connection, and navigating life together

● Wanting to build skills for long-term growth and stability

Therapy That Supports Growth and Empowerment

Shaquoi’s goal is to create a space where clients feel respected, supported, and confident in their ability to grow.

Through therapy, clients can begin to:

● Feel more seen, heard, and understood

● Build confidence in themselves and their decisions

● Develop tools to manage life’s challenges

● Create a more balanced, fulfilling life

Her approach is grounded in authenticity, empathy, and a genuine commitment to each client’s journey.

Start Therapy In Medina, Ohio or via Telehealth

Shaquoi offers therapy both in-person (Medina office) and via telehealth, providing flexible options for individuals seeking supportive, inclusive care.

If you’re ready to explore your experiences, build new skills, and move toward your goals, this may be a strong fit.
