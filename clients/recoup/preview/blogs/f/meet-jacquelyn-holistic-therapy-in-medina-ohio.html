Meet Jacquelyn: Holistic Therapy in Medina, Ohio

July 13, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist isn’t just about talking through challenges. It’s about working with someone who helps you create real, lasting change in how you live, think, and show up every day.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Jacquelyn provides therapy in Medina, Ohio and via telehealth for adults navigating burnout, life transitions, emotional challenges, and performance-related stress. Her approach is holistic, action-oriented, and grounded in helping clients build sustainable change through intentional, values-driven choices.

Moving Beyond Feeling Stuck or Burned Out

Many people come to therapy feeling overwhelmed, unmotivated, or disconnected from the life they want to be living.

You might be:

● Experiencing burnout or chronic stress

● Struggling with mood fluctuations or emotional regulation

● Navigating a major life transition

● Feeling stuck in patterns that no longer serve you

● Trying to balance performance, expectations, and personal well-being

Jacquelyn works with clients to move beyond just managing these experiences and instead create meaningful, forward movement.

Together, you’ll begin to:

● Identify patterns that are holding you back

● Build motivation and clarity around your goals

● Develop tools to regulate emotions and manage stress

● Create sustainable habits that support long-term wellness

A Holistic, Mind-Body Approach to Change

Jacquelyn’s work is rooted in a holistic understanding of mental health, recognizing that emotional well-being, physical experience, and daily habits are all deeply connected.

She integrates a range of approaches, including:

● Mindfulness-based techniques to build awareness and presence

● Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to shift unhelpful thought patterns

● DBT-informed skills for emotional regulation

● Somatic practices to connect with the body and process stress

● Reality therapy to support action and accountability

This combination allows therapy to feel both grounded and actionable. Helping clients not just understand their challenges, but actively work through them.

Empowerment Through Action and Intentional Living

At the core of Jacquelyn’s approach is the belief that meaningful change happens through intentional, values-driven action.

Therapy isn’t just about insight. It’s about applying that insight in real, tangible ways.

Jacquelyn helps clients:

● Strengthen their sense of personal agency

● Align their actions with their values

● Build consistency in habits and routines

● Develop confidence in their ability to create change

Her work focuses on helping clients move from feeling stuck to feeling capable and in control of their direction.

Supporting Performance, Identity, and Growth

Jacquelyn has a unique background working across clinical, community, and performance-focused environments, including experience with athletes and individuals navigating high expectations.

She understands the pressure that can come with:

● Performance and achievement

● Identity and self-expectations

● Balancing ambition with well-being

Her own involvement in creative performance spaces also informs her approach, bringing an appreciation for discipline, expression, and joy as important parts of growth and healing.

Who Jacquelyn Works Best With

Jacquelyn works with adults who are ready to take an active role in their growth and well-being. She also specializes in men’s issues and high-performing athletes.

She is a strong fit for individuals who are:

● Experiencing burnout, stress, or emotional overwhelm

● Navigating life transitions or identity shifts

● Struggling with mood regulation or motivation

● Managing substance use or lifestyle-related challenges

● Looking to improve performance (athletic, professional, or personal)

● Seeking a holistic, action-oriented approach to therapy

Therapy That Creates Sustainable Change

Jacquelyn is passionate about helping clients build a life that feels both fulfilling and sustainable.

Her approach combines compassion with accountability. Creating a space where you can:

● Feel supported and understood

● Build practical tools for daily life

● Strengthen resilience and emotional awareness

● Create meaningful, lasting change

If you’re ready to move beyond feeling stuck and start creating real change, this may be a strong fit.
