Meet Cathy: Trauma-Informed Therapy in Medina

August 5, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist isn’t just about working through challenges. It’s about having someone who understands the realities of your experiences and can support you through meaningful life transitions.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Cathy provides therapy in Medina, Ohio and via telehealth, bringing more than 30 years of experience working with children, families, and young adults. Her approach is grounded in trauma-informed care, advocacy, and helping clients build stability, confidence, and independence through life’s challenges.

Support Through Life’s Most Difficult Transitions

Many people come to therapy during periods of uncertainty or change.

You might be:

● Navigating a major life transition

● Processing past trauma or difficult experiences

● Adjusting to new roles or responsibilities

● Trying to build stability after a challenging period

● Feeling unsure of how to move forward

Cathy’s work is centered on helping clients feel supported and grounded during these moments.

Together, you’ll begin to:

● Process past experiences in a safe, supportive space

● Identify current challenges and areas of growth

● Build practical life skills and coping strategies

● Develop a stronger sense of direction and stability

A Trauma-Informed, Advocacy-Focused Approach

Cathy’s career has been deeply rooted in working with individuals and families in complex systems, including child welfare and community-based support.

Her experience includes:

● Supporting survivors and families in crisis

● Working closely with children and families to ensure safety and well-being

● Helping youth in foster care and young adults build independence and life skills

● Leading programs focused on long-term stability and transition into adulthood

This background allows her to approach therapy with a deep understanding of how trauma, systems, and life circumstances intersect.

Her work is grounded in:

● Trauma-informed care

● Advocacy and empowerment

● Strengths-based support

● Practical, real-world application

Building Stability, Confidence, and Independence

Cathy is especially passionate about helping clients develop the tools they need to move forward with confidence.

Her work often focuses on:

● Life skills and independent living support

● Emotional resilience and coping strategies

● Building structure and stability

● Navigating relationships and support systems

● Strengthening confidence during periods of change

She understands that growth doesn’t happen all at once. It’s built through consistent support, small steps, and a safe space to learn and adjust along the way.

Who Cathy Works Best With

Cathy works with individuals who are navigating transitions, trauma, or periods of instability and are looking for grounded, supportive care.

She is a strong fit for clients who are:

● Processing past trauma or difficult life experiences

● Navigating life transitions or major changes

● Seeking support with independence and life skills

● Adjusting to new environments or roles

● Looking for a therapist who brings both experience and compassion

Therapy That Meets You Where You Are

Cathy’s approach is rooted in meeting clients where they are—without judgment, pressure, or unrealistic expectations.

Her goal is to create a space where you can:

● Feel safe and supported

● Build confidence in your ability to move forward

● Develop practical tools for everyday life

● Work toward a more stable and sustainable future

Start Therapy in Medina, Ohio or via Telehealth

Cathy offers therapy in Medina, Ohio and via telehealth for individuals seeking trauma-informed, supportive care through life’s transitions.

If you’re looking for guidance, stability, and a therapist who understands the complexity of your experiences, this may be a strong fit.

Call 330-636-5000 or email hello@recoupandreliefcounseling.com to start working with Cathy.
