Meet Suzanne: ADHD & Executive Function Support in Dublin, Ohio

July 27, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist for ADHD is about more than learning tips or staying organized. It’s about working with someone who genuinely understands how ADHD shows up in real life and what it takes to make meaningful, lasting change.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Suzanne provides ADHD therapy in Dublin, Ohio and via telehealth. Her approach is grounded in both personal understanding and clinical experience, helping clients navigate the challenges of ADHD while building confidence, structure, and a stronger sense of self.

Understanding ADHD Beyond the Surface

ADHD is often reduced to a focus problem, but for many people, it touches nearly every part of daily life.

You might experience:

● Trouble starting or finishing tasks

● Feeling overwhelmed by simple responsibilities

● Emotional highs and lows that feel hard to control

● Difficulty staying organized or managing time

● Patterns of procrastination, burnout, or frustration

ADHD also commonly overlaps with anxiety, depression, and low self-esteem, particularly when these challenges have been misunderstood or overlooked for years.

Suzanne helps clients make sense of these experiences in a way that feels validating, not overwhelming.

Support That Actually Fits Your Brain

Suzanne has spent her life surrounded by individuals with ADHD, giving her a deep, authentic understanding of the neurodiverse experience.

She recognizes that traditional approaches don’t always work for neurodiverse clients, and that what most people need isn’t more pressure or stricter routines, but a different framework for understanding how their brain operates.

Her work focuses on:

● Executive functioning (planning, organization, follow-through)

● Emotional regulation

● Communication skills

● Building confidence and self-trust

● Creating systems that actually work in real life

Rather than forcing rigid structure, Suzanne helps clients develop tools and strategies that align with how they naturally function.

Building Skills, Confidence, and Self-Understanding

ADHD can have a significant impact on how people see themselves, and many clients arrive feeling frustrated, stuck, or like they’re constantly falling short, even when they’re putting in genuine effort. Suzanne works to shift that narrative, helping clients move toward a more grounded and

compassionate understanding of themselves.

Through therapy, clients begin to:

● Understand how ADHD is influencing their patterns

● Build practical systems to manage daily life

● Strengthen emotional awareness and regulation

● Improve communication and relationships

● Develop a more confident and compassionate view of themselves

Her approach is both practical and empowering, with a focus on helping clients feel more capable rather than more overwhelmed.

A Personalized, Collaborative Approach

Because no two people experience ADHD in the same way, Suzanne’s work is highly individualized, with treatment plans tailored to each client’s needs, goals, and lifestyle. Sessions are collaborative by design, with a shared focus on identifying what’s getting in the way and building strategies that are realistic and sustainable over time.

In sessions, you can expect:

● A supportive, nonjudgmental space

● Collaborative problem-solving

● Strategies that are realistic and sustainable

● A focus on both immediate improvements and long-term growth

Who Suzanne Works Best With

Suzanne works with individuals navigating ADHD and related challenges who are looking for support that feels both practical and validating.

She is a strong fit for clients who are:

● Struggling with ADHD symptoms or executive functioning

● Feeling overwhelmed, disorganized, or stuck in cycles of procrastination

● Experiencing anxiety or depression alongside ADHD

● Looking to improve emotional regulation and communication

● Wanting to better understand themselves and how their brain works

● Seeking an affirming, supportive space within the LGBTQI+ community

Therapy That Helps You Move Forward

Suzanne’s goal is to help clients build a life that feels manageable and fulfilling, rather than one shaped by constant stress or self-doubt. Through a combination of understanding, strategy, and support, she helps clients move toward

a place where they feel:

● More confident in their abilities

● More in control of their day-to-day life

● More connected to who they truly are
