Meet Jessica: Trauma-Informed & Affirming Therapy in Dublin, Ohio

July 26, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist isn’t just about having someone to talk to. It’s about feeling safe, understood, and supported as you work through life’s challenges.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Jessica provides therapy in Dublin, Ohio and via telehealth, offering a compassionate, nonjudgmental space for individuals navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, identity exploration, and life transitions. Her approach is person-centered, collaborative, and grounded in helping clients build resilience, self-compassion, and meaningful change.

A Safe Space to Start Where You Are

Starting therapy can feel overwhelming, especially if you’re carrying experiences that have been difficult to process or put into words.

You might be:

● Working through past trauma or emotional pain

● Navigating anxiety or depression

● Exploring identity or gender expression

● Feeling stuck in your career or unsure of your next step

● Questioning patterns that don’t seem to change

Jessica meets clients exactly where they are. Without pressure, judgment, or expectation.

Together, you’ll begin to:

● Process experiences at your own pace

● Build awareness around thoughts and emotions

● Develop tools to navigate daily challenges

● Move toward greater clarity and stability

A Person-Centered, Collaborative Approach

Jessica’s work is rooted in the belief that therapy should feel like a partnership.

She creates a space where clients feel heard, respected, and actively involved in their own growth.

Her approach focuses on:

● Building self-compassion and reducing self-criticism

● Strengthening accountability and personal agency

● Supporting emotional regulation and resilience

● Creating realistic, meaningful goals

By combining empathy with gentle guidance, Jessica helps clients move forward in a way that feels both supported and empowering.

Trauma-Informed and Mindfulness-Based Care

Jessica integrates trauma-informed care and mindfulness techniques to support healing and growth.

Her work allows clients to:

● Safely process past experiences

● Stay present and grounded during difficult moments

● Build a deeper connection to themselves

● Develop healthier coping strategies

This approach creates a balance between understanding the past and building tools for the present.

Affirming Support for Identity and Neurodivergence

Jessica is especially passionate about supporting individuals exploring identity, including LGBTQIA+ clients and those seeking gender-affirming care.

She also works with women who may be exploring or identifying potential neurodivergence, helping them better understand themselves and their experiences.

Her approach is affirming, inclusive, and grounded in:

● Respect for each client’s identity and lived experience

● Support for self-discovery and personal expression

● Creating a space where clients feel safe to be fully themselves

Who Jessica Works Best With

Jessica works with individuals who are looking for a supportive, affirming, and insight-driven approach to therapy.

She is a strong fit for clients who are:

● Processing trauma or past experiences

● Experiencing anxiety or depression

● Exploring identity or seeking LGBTQIA+-affirming care

● Navigating career questions or life direction

● Exploring possible neurodivergence or self-understanding

● Looking to build self-compassion and resilience

Therapy That Builds Confidence and Clarity

Jessica’s goal is to help clients feel more connected to themselves and more confident in how they move through the world.

Through therapy, clients can begin to:

● Develop a stronger sense of self

● Build resilience and emotional awareness

● Navigate challenges with greater clarity

● Create meaningful, sustainable change

Start Therapy in Dublin, Ohio or via Telehealth

Jessica offers therapy in Dublin, Ohio and via telehealth for individuals seeking trauma-informed, affirming, and supportive care.

If you’re ready to explore your experiences, build self-understanding, and move forward with greater confidence, this may be a strong fit.
