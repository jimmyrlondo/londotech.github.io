Meet Gabrielle: Therapy for Life Transitions & Identity in Medina

June 18, 2026 · Recoup + Relief

Finding the right therapist is about more than learning coping skills, it’s about finding someone

who understands the complexity of your experiences and helps you make sense of them in a

meaningful way.

At Recoup + Relief Counseling, Gabrielle offers therapy in Dublin, Ohio for adults navigating

anxiety, life transitions, relationship challenges, and questions of identity and belonging. Her

approach is thoughtful, collaborative, and grounded in both clinical expertise and cultural

awareness.

Looking Beyond Feeling “Stuck”

Many people seek therapy not because they’re in crisis, but because something feels off.

You might feel overwhelmed, disconnected, or caught in patterns that don’t fully make sense.

Maybe you’re balancing multiple roles, navigating expectations from family or culture, or trying

to figure out who you are outside of what’s been expected of you.

Gabrielle helps clients slow down and take a closer look at what’s underneath those

experiences.

Together, you’ll begin to explore:

● Patterns in thoughts, emotions, and relationships

● The impact of cultural expectations and identity

● Internal pressures and competing roles

● How past experiences may still be shaping the present

The goal isn’t to simplify your experience, it’s to better understand it so you can move forward

with more clarity and intention.

A Space to Explore Identity and Belonging

For many of Gabrielle’s clients, therapy is a space to explore deeper questions around identity,

culture, and belonging.

She frequently works with:

● First-generation Americans

● Immigrants and multicultural individuals

● Creative professionals navigating identity and career

● Individuals balancing multiple roles, expectations, and responsibilities

● Couples seeking support with communication, connection, and navigating life together

Gabrielle understands that these experiences often come with unspoken pressure, whether it’s

meeting expectations, navigating different cultural norms, or defining your own path.

Her approach creates space for honest conversation about these realities, without judgment or

the need to “fit” into a simplified narrative.

Therapy That Is Collaborative, Direct, and Grounded

Gabrielle’s style is both supportive and direct. She works collaboratively with clients while also

helping them gently challenge patterns that may be keeping them stuck.

Her work integrates:

● Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) to understand thought patterns

● DBT-informed skills to strengthen emotional regulation

● Narrative therapy to explore personal identity and meaning

● Relational approaches to understand patterns in relationships

This combination allows therapy to feel both practical and reflective, helping clients not only

understand themselves more deeply, but also make real, sustainable changes.

Creativity, Culture, and the Bigger Picture

With a background in fine arts and long-standing involvement in creative communities, Gabrielle

brings a unique lens to therapy.

She understands how creativity, identity, work, and emotional well-being often intersect,

especially for those navigating nontraditional paths or high internal expectations.

She is also deeply attuned to the emotional weight of:

● Navigating cultural expectations

● Balancing responsibility and self-definition

● Feeling pulled between different identities or roles

This perspective allows her to meet clients with nuance, curiosity, and a deep respect for their

full experience.

Who Gabrielle Works Best With

Gabrielle works with adults who are looking for a thoughtful, insight-driven approach to therapy.

She is a good fit for individuals who are:

● Experiencing anxiety or ongoing stress

● Navigating life transitions or identity shifts

● Working through relationship challenges

● Exploring questions of identity, culture, and belonging

● Feeling overwhelmed, disconnected, or stuck in patterns

● Balancing creative work, professional demands, and personal expectations

Therapy That Honors Your Full Experience

Gabrielle believes that therapy should feel real, grounded, and reflective of who you actually

are, not a simplified version.

Her goal is to create a space where you can:

● Explore complexity without pressure

● Build clarity and self-understanding

● Strengthen emotional resilience

● Move toward a more integrated and sustainable way of living
