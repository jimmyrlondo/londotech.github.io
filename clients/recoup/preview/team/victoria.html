Our therapists

Victoria, MA, AT, ATR-P, LPC

Art Therapy + Counselor

Hi I’m Victoria! I graduated from Ursuline College with a Master of Arts degree in Counseling and Art Therapy. I’m an art therapist and a licensed professional counselor and am available for in-person as well as virtual sessions.

I have experience working with children ages 5 to 17, as well as adults to age 55, presenting with anxiety, depression, low self-esteem, anger, adjustment issues, trauma, self-harm, ADHD, addiction recovery, and Autism spectrum related concerns. I have worked in inpatient and outpatient settings with both groups and individuals.

I take a compassionate approach to mental health and pull from different counseling theories, collaborating with clients every step of the way to address their concerns and meet their needs. I like to utilize the following approaches CBT, DBT, Art Therapy, Expressive Arts, Feminist, Humanistic, Narrative, Person-centered, strength-based and trauma focused approaches.

Humans have an innate need to be creative. I believe in the healing power of art and want to share it with everyone! Victoria practices under the direct supervision of Dereka Feiler, MSSA, LISW-S.
