Our therapists

Dereka, MSSA, LISW-S

Owner and Clinical Director

Hi! I'm Dereka. I'm a Licensed Independent Social Worker and I earned my Bachelor of Social Work from Youngstown State University and Master of Science in Social Administration from the Mandel School of Applied Social Sciences at Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland, Ohio. I am the Clinical Director and Founder of Recoup + Relief Counseling & Therapeutic Services, LLC.

I have over 8 years of experience working with multi-system youth; including those involved with Child Protective Services. I've worked with children and families during their time in foster care as well as transitioning to adoption. I've worked with children on the

Autism Spectrum to help integrate social skills via group therapy. Additionally, I have also worked in the school and hospital settings. I have experience working with a wide variety of clients and ages. Together, we can work through many familial issues involving living situations, relationships and communication. I also have experience addressing anxiety, substance abuse, stress management, mood disorders, traumatic experiences, family conflict, pregnancy, infertility and transitions into parenthood.

I have training in Trauma Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Dialectical Behavioral Therapy for the treatment of anxiety and depression in children, adolescents, and family systems. I utilize these modalities to treat anxiety, depression, trauma, suicidal ideations.

My hope is that you will feel comfortable to be open and honest throughout the process of therapy. I will offer you a safe space where you can freely share your honest thoughts and fears without judgement. My goal is for you to feel more connected to yourself and those around you, improve your relationships and be more present in everyday life. My therapy style is easy-going, open-minded and relaxed. I am intuitive, inquisitive and empathetic. Together, we will collaborate to get you to where you want to be. Throughout our time working together, I want you to feel confident, aligned and mindful of where you're headed in your journey.

I want you to leave each session feeling recouped and relieved.

I love spending time and spoiling my two dachshunds.

Hulk and Dozer. They keep me busy!

When I'm not working, I enjoy trying new restaurants and eating delicious food. I love interior design, a good piece of furniture and transforming spaces.
