Our therapists

Erika, LPCC

Clinical Counselor

a Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor to our practice. She will be seeing clients at our Medina office both in person and via telehealth.

Erika earned her Master’s in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from Cleveland State University in 2020 and has since dedicated her career to supporting adolescents and adults through life’s challenges, transitions, and emotional struggles with compassion, clarity, and understanding.

She has a strong passion for helping individuals process and heal from trauma and is currently working toward EMDR certification. Erika also brings valuable experience working with individuals impacted by substance use, those involved in the criminal justice system, as well as first responders and veterans. Her approach centers on creating a safe, collaborative, and authentic environment where clients feel supported to explore, grow, and reconnect with their inner strength.

Erika draws from a wide range of evidence-based modalities including EMDR, Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), CBT for Insomnia (CBT-I), Trauma-Focused CBT (TF-CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), Flash Technique, Motivational Interviewing, IFS-informed work, and Trauma-informed care, which are all tailored to each person’s unique story, goals, and pace.

She works with individuals ages 14 and older, focusing on trauma, anxiety, depression, major life changes and stressors, college transitions, dissociation, BPD, addictions and relapse prevention, bipolar disorders.

Erika is on a waitlist at this time.
