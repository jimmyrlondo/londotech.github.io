Our therapists

Cathy, MSSA, LSW

Therapist

I am a dedicated social work professional with more than three decades of experience serving children, families and youth in the child welfare system. I earned my undergrad degree from Cleveland State in 1992 and began my career at a domestic violence shelter where I provided direct support to survivors and families in crisis. I later joined Lorain County Children Services in October, 1994. I served as an ongoing caseworker for six years, working closely with families and children to ensure safety, permanence and well being. I then transitioned to the Independent Living Program where I supported youth in foster care and young adults who had aged out, helping them build life skills, stability and independence through the age of 21.

In 1998, I received my Masters of Social Sciences Administration from Case Western Reserve. In 2020, I became the supervisor of the Independent Living Program, a role I continue to hold today. My work is grounded in advocacy, trauma-informed practice, and a deep commitment to empower people as they transition through different challenges in their life.
