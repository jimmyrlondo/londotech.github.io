Our therapists

Jessica, LPCC

Clinical Counselor

Jessica provides a safe, non-judgmental, and welcoming space for clients to share their experiences, begin healing, and work toward overcoming life’s challenges.

Jessica meets clients where they are, offering empathetic listening and a collaborative approach to help individuals reach their therapeutic goals. She focuses on building self-compassion, accountability, and resilience. She enjoys working from a person-focused lens utilizing mindfulness techniques and trauma-informed care.

Her areas of expertise include supporting those who have experienced trauma, depression, anxiety, LGBTQIA+ individuals and gender-affirming care, career counseling for adults, and assisting women in identifying potential neurodivergence.
