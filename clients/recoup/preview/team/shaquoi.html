Shaquoi, M.Ed, CMCH, LPC

Counselor

Hi! I’m Shaquoi. I’m a Licensed Professional Counselor. I earned my Master of Education in Clinical Mental Health Counseling from Cleveland State University. I earned my Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from Mississippi College.

While at Cleveland State, I placed specific focus on my development to become a LGBTQIA+ affirming and culturally competent therapist. I’m able to work with a wide variety of presenting concerns, including trauma, gender issues, affectional/sexuality orientation issues, self-esteem and depression. I have clinical experience working with individuals, couples, adolescents and children. I also have experience in working with young adults at a collegiate counseling center.

My aim is to create a comfortable, unbiased, safe space where the client’s goals are prioritized. My clients can expect to be met with support, respect, empathy and the confidence in their ability to cultivate positive change in their lives. I believe each individual is the expert in their lives and therapy is a collaboration between the client and therapist.

I have experience utilizing Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and Acceptance and Commitment Therapy. As a lifelong learner, I’m excited to expand my skills and therapeutic expertise through continuing education in various theoretic modalities.

My hope is to build a strong therapeutic relationship where we will explore your concerns and celebrate your successes. My goal is to provide support where you feel respected, seen, and heard while developing skills to maintain a healthy life and overcome challenges. My commitment is to meet each individual with authenticity and empathy as we work towards accomplishing your goals.

When not working, I enjoy spending time with my family, walking local trails and taking yoga classes.

Shaquoi practices under the direct supervision of Dereka Feiler, MSSA, LISW-S.

Availability: Monday, Tuesday + Thursday evenings, Saturday + Sunday morning/afternoon
