Our therapists

Gabrielle, MSW, BFA, LSW

Therapist

Gabrielle is a Licensed Social Worker who provides individual, couples, and family therapy for adults navigating anxiety, life transitions, relationship challenges, and questions of identity and belonging. Many of the people she works with are first-generation Americans, immigrants, multicultural individuals, and creative professionals balancing multiple roles, expectations, and internal pressures.

She holds a Bachelor of Fine Arts from the Cleveland Institute of Art and a Master of Social Work from Cleveland State University, where her clinical training focused on trauma-informed care, grief and loss, and culturally responsive practice. Her background across both creative and clinical fields informs a thoughtful, nuanced approach to therapy.

Gabrielle’s work is collaborative, direct, and grounded in both evidence-based practice and cultural awareness. She integrates Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), DBT-informed skills, narrative therapy, and relational frameworks to help clients better understand patterns, strengthen emotional regulation, and build more sustainable ways of showing up in their lives and relationships.

She understands that many clients are not in crisis, but still feel overwhelmed, disconnected, or stuck in repeating cycles. Therapy with Gabrielle creates space for honest conversation, reflection, and meaningful change, without judgment or pressure to simplify who you are.

With a background in community-based work and long-standing engagement with artists and creative communities, Gabrielle brings a nuanced understanding of how identity, work, family, and creativity intersect. She is especially attuned to the emotional weight of navigating cultural expectations, responsibility, and self-definition.

She is committed to creating a therapy space that feels grounded, thoughtful, and real, where clients can explore complexity, build clarity, and move toward a more integrated and sustainable sense of self.
