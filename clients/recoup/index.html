Month 1

Fix the front door and start measuring

Make it easy and private for someone to request an appointment, and start counting every request.

Londo Technologies will

  • Connect Google Analytics and Google Search Console, so we can see real visitors, what they searched, and where they drop off.
  • Replace the email “Schedule” buttons with one secure request form, ideally your scheduling system’s own booking widget.
  • Build an insurance and fees page, and add a one-line answer to the homepage.
  • Label who is accepting new clients, and give waitlisted clinicians a “match me with someone available” option.
  • Fix GoDaddy’s SEO settings: real business locations, plus Dublin and telehealth keywords.
  • Review GoDaddy’s pending SEO suggestions page by page, instead of approving all.

From you

  • Insurance plans accepted and self-pay rates.
  • Which scheduling system you use, and admin access to it.
  • Who owns new requests, and a response-time promise (for example, one business day).
  • Access to GoDaddy and Google accounts.
You get: every appointment request arrives privately, gets counted, and gets answered.
2
Months 2 to 3

Get found locally

Show up when people near Medina and Dublin, or anywhere in Ohio for telehealth, search for a therapist, not just for your name.

Londo Technologies will

  • Claim, verify and fully build out Google Business Profiles for Medina and Dublin: categories, services, hours, photos.
  • Make your name, address and phone match everywhere: the site, Google, Psychology Today and other directories.
  • Build an Ohio-wide telehealth page.
  • Build service pages that pair a specialty with a city, like “child therapy in Medina” or “EMDR therapy in Dublin.”
  • Set up a simple review link and an ethical process for asking for reviews.

From you

  • Help with Google’s verification at each office (a postcard or phone call).
  • A few photos of each office.
  • Share the review link with former clients and referral partners. Current clients should never be asked directly.
  • Decide whether Dublin should get a local Columbus-area phone number.
You get: a real presence on Google Maps and local search for both offices and telehealth.
3
Months 4 to 6

Build authority

Give Google more reasons to trust the site, and give visitors more reasons to pick your therapists.

Londo Technologies will

  • Plan blog posts around the questions local people actually search, each one linking to the matching service page.
  • Rework therapist bios into search pages: specialties, insurance, location, and a “request this therapist” button.
  • Reach out for links from referral partners: schools, pediatricians, OB/GYN offices, foster and adoption agencies.
  • Reconnect Facebook and set up a steady posting rhythm that points back to the site.

From you

  • A clinician’s quick review of each post before it goes live.
  • Updated bio details from each therapist.
  • A list of referral partners you already work with.
You get: steady growth in people who find you by searching for help, not by name.

What we will fix

What is holding the site back, and when we fix it

The practice has real strengths: 18 clinicians, most accepting new clients, a wide range of specialties, and solid pages for Medina and Dublin. The gaps are almost all in the last step, where an interested visitor tries to become a client.

Phase 1

Every “Schedule” button opens an email draft, not a form or booking calendar.

On phones and work computers this often opens nothing. People give up silently, and nobody can count how many tried.

Phase 1

The booking email asks for name, date of birth, phone and insurance.

That is health-adjacent personal data going through regular email, and a lot to type before anyone has answered a question.

Phase 1

No insurance list on the site.

“Do you take my insurance?” is the first question most people have. With no answer, they move on.

Phase 1

Waitlisted clinicians use the same booking buttons.

Someone who picks a waitlisted therapist may not hear back quickly and assume the practice is full.

Phase 1

GoDaddy’s SEO settings target Medina only, and the location is set to “US.”

Nothing tells Google about Dublin or telehealth, which is likely why no Dublin searches show up.

Phase 2

Telehealth is used a lot, but has no page of its own.

Anyone in Ohio can be a telehealth client, but people searching for online therapy have nowhere to land.

Phase 2

One phone number (330, Medina area code) for both offices.

A Dublin searcher sees a Northeast Ohio number. A local number builds trust and helps local search.

Phase 2

No reviews or trust signals on the homepage.

Visitors have nothing to reassure them before reaching out. Google reviews and credentials do this job.

To get started

A few answers from you, and we begin

These decide where phase 1 starts. Most take a few minutes.

  • Roughly how many new clients do you start per month today, and how do most of them say they found you?
  • How many emailed appointment requests arrive in a typical week, who answers them, and how fast?
  • Which scheduling or records system do you use (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes, other)?
  • Which insurance plans do you accept, and what are your self-pay rates?
  • Which clients do you most want more of (children and teens, couples, trauma, perinatal, foster and adoptive families)?
  • Where is there the most open capacity right now: Medina, Dublin, or telehealth?
  • Roughly what share of sessions are telehealth, and where in Ohio do those clients live?
  • Do you have Google Business Profiles for each office, and who has the login?
  • Who has access to the GoDaddy, Google, Facebook and Psychology Today accounts?
  • Is hiring part of the goal? If not, “Join the Team” moves from the main menu to the footer.
