Common questions

Can you get me out of my current lease?

Often, yes. Send us your current lease and we will look at the buyout and what it would cost to switch. Many customers end up paying less each month even after the buyout.

Do you only service the brands you sell?

No. We are an authorized Konica Minolta, Kyocera and Sharp dealer, and our technicians are trained on all major brands, including Canon, Ricoh, Savin, Lanier, Toshiba and Xerox.

How fast is service?

We are a local company, so a technician is usually much closer than a national dealer's. Call us and we will give you a real time, not a ticket number.

What does a service contract cover?

Toner, parts, labor and travel for one simple per-copy rate. You pay for what you print and nothing else.

Paying too much for your copier?

Send us your current lease or your last bill. We will tell you in plain numbers whether we can save you money. No pressure, no obligation.
