Managed print

Never run out of toner again

Running out of toner, or a printer going down at the worst moment, slows the whole office. Managed print puts us on top of it, so supplies arrive before you need them and problems get fixed before you notice.

  1. We check your fleet

    We look at every copier and printer you have, what each one costs to run, and which ones you could do without.

  2. Machines report in

    Your devices securely report toner levels, page counts and error codes to us automatically.

  3. Supplies just show up

    Toner ships before a cartridge runs dry. No ordering, no stockroom full of the wrong cartridges.

  4. One simple bill

    Pay per page across the whole office, with service included. Easy to budget, easy to approve.

Where the savings come from

  • Fewer emergency orders. No more paying rush prices for toner from a big-box store.
  • Less downtime. We often know about a problem before your staff calls.
  • The right machine for the job. Moving printing off expensive desktop inkjets onto your copier lowers the cost of every page.
  • Less time wasted. Nobody on your staff has to babysit printers.
