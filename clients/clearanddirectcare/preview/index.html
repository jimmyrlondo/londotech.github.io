How it works

Three steps, no fine print.

  1. Choose your membership

    Pick the plan that fits your life, your goals and how much support you want. Every plan includes concierge scheduling, and select plans can be shared with people you aren't related to.

  2. Start where you need to

    Book lab work, an urgent care visit, counseling, nutrition or physical therapy, depending on your plan. Our concierge team handles the scheduling and coordination.

  3. Know what you pay

    A set monthly rate, with a discount for paying annually. No guessing, no surprise bills, no waiting on insurance to say yes.
