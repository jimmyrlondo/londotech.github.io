Frequently asked

Good to know

When is the concierge team available?

Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m., excluding holidays. We respond to emails and inquiries within 12 business hours, and phone follow-ups typically happen within one business day.

What should I do in a medical emergency?

Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department immediately for chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe bleeding, loss of consciousness or suicidal thoughts. Clear and Direct Care does not provide emergency services.

What if I'm having a mental health crisis?

Call or text 988 immediately. It is free, confidential and available 24 hours a day.

How do I get a copy of my medical records?

Request them through the patient portal or by email. We provide access within 72 hours at the latest. With your written authorization, we can also share records with your other providers.

What happens if I'm running late to an appointment?

Let us know as soon as you can. Depending on the time available, your appointment may go ahead as scheduled, be shortened or need to be rescheduled.

How do I share a concern or complaint?

Email info@clearanddirectcare.com. Our leadership team reviews every concern and works toward a timely, respectful resolution.

Can a membership be ended?

Clear and Direct Care may suspend or end a membership for non-payment, fraud, abusive behavior, policy violations, or when the provider-patient relationship can no longer be maintained safely or effectively.
