About us

Your health. Their priority.

We're parents and business owners who got tired of a healthcare system that wasn't built with real people in mind. So we built the one we wanted.

A better approach to healthcare

Too many people delay getting help because they can't predict the cost, can't navigate the system, or simply don't know where to start. They wait for things to get worse. They avoid the doctor. They piece together care that was never designed to work together, and somehow it still costs a fortune.

We started Clear and Direct Care because we believed there was a better way. Not better in a complicated, high-tech, hard-to-explain way. Better in the most straightforward sense: you know what you're paying, you get the care you need, and it comes to you.

Our membership model strips away the confusion of traditional insurance. No surprise bills. No referral runarounds. No sitting in a waiting room for 45 minutes to be seen for 10. Instead you get a care team that actually shows up, in your home and on your schedule: urgent care, physical therapy, nutrition, mental health and lab work, all coordinated in one place.

Healthcare doesn't have to be a system you fight against. It can just be clear and direct.

Leadership

The people who decided healthcare could be better, and built it.

Dereka Feiler

Co-founder & CEO

Dereka is an entrepreneur, licensed social worker and mother who knows firsthand how frustrating it is to navigate a healthcare system that wasn't built with real people in mind. That frustration became fuel. As co-founder of Clear and Direct Care, she has turned it into real solutions, building the kind of care she wished had always existed.

Dominic Berardinelli

Co-founder & CFO

Dominic is a franchise owner, husband and father. After leaving his corporate career to own a franchise, he ran into a healthcare system that wasn't good enough for his family: confusing options, zero transparency and coverage that didn't deliver. That was the catalyst for co-creating Clear and Direct Care, so people can finally have the care they value.

Care team

MobileMD

In-home urgent care

When urgent care can't wait, MobileMD brings a licensed clinician directly to your door. Their team of experienced Family Nurse Practitioners, backed by a physician, delivers the same quality care you'd expect in a clinic, without the waiting room, the surprise bills, or the stress. MobileMD was founded by Darryl Costin Jr., a Cleveland native who believes in quick, quality care.

The Therapy Collective

Mental health therapists

An Ohio-based practice specializing in ADHD, anxiety, depression, and trauma. Their licensed therapists share our belief that getting the right support shouldn't be complicated. Now that support is one step closer to your door.

Any Lab Test Now

Labs

Direct access to hundreds of lab tests: no doctor's order required, no insurance hassle, and no waiting weeks for answers. Fast, affordable, transparent lab work that puts you in control of your own health data.

Amanda Liptak, RDN

Lead nutritionist

Amanda, founder of Nutrient Rich Life and a Registered Dietitian Nutritionist with more than 20 years of experience, helps people uncover the root causes behind their health challenges. She specializes in functional and integrative nutrition, combining science-based strategies with personalized care to improve energy, gut health, metabolism and hormones through practical, sustainable habits.

Amy McMillin, PT

Lead physical therapist

Amy, owner of Custom Care Rehab, has been improving patient outcomes in Northeast Ohio since 2007. She has advanced specialty training in vestibular disorders, vertigo, migraines and concussions, including dry needling and deep tissue work, and brings a level of expertise to your home that most patients never get in a traditional clinic.
