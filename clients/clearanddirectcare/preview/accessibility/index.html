Accessibility statement

Clear and Direct Care wants everyone to be able to learn about and use our services, including people who use assistive technology.

Our standard

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. That includes:

  • Page structure with clear, ordered headings and a "skip to content" link
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images
  • Color combinations that meet contrast requirements
  • Form fields with visible labels
  • Respect for your device's reduced-motion setting
  • Layouts that work when zoomed and on small screens

Third-party content

Membership checkout and the patient portal are provided by outside vendors, and their accessibility depends partly on those vendors. If anything there gets in your way, contact us and we'll help you complete it another way.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, email info@clearanddirectcare.com with the page and what happened. We'll respond within 12 business hours and work with you on a fix or an alternative.
