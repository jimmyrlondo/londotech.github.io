Release your music

From finished mix to release. Take the whole journey in one place, or use any step on its own.

Journey NEW→Drop your songs once and take them all the way: master them so they sound like one release, add titles, credits and cover art, check everything with Last Call, and download every format you need in one zip.Master→Master a finished mix: pick a sound, check your mix, compare with a song you love, hear it the way Spotify and Apple will play it, and export tagged, release-ready files.Live→Record a show or drop in the recording. It finds the songs, masters them all evenly, and gets the live album ready to sell.Last Call→The check before you release: loudness on every streaming service, clipping, mono, clicks at the start and end, format, tags and cover art, with plain fixes.Convert→Drop a file, get an MP3, WAV, FLAC, Apple Lossless, AAC or Opus with its tags and cover kept. Reads DSD and old formats too. One song or a whole folder.
ENCORE.

Get it heard

Your song is finished. Now get it heard, and get paid for it.

Clips NEW→Drop in your song and cover. It finds the hook and makes vertical videos for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, a Spotify Canvas loop and more, with your lyrics on screen.Links NEW→Paste one link and get a release page with every streaming service, a countdown and pre-save buttons, an email sign-up for your own list and a QR code. Free, no monthly fee.Credits NEW→Every song's codes, credits and splits in one place, with split sheets to sign and a checklist of where to register so no royalty gets left behind.
ENCORE.

Write and play

For the practice room, the stage and the studio.

Songbook NEW→Write lyrics with chords and keep them in project folders. Tap a chord to drop it in, paste any chord sheet and tidy it in one tap, see how to play every chord on any instrument, find rhymes and the right word, and copy it anywhere.Compose NEW→Write sheet music: click the staff, type note names, or play a MIDI keyboard. Record with a click and it writes out what you played. Chords, lyrics, any instrument, playback, and MusicXML, MIDI and PDF out.Tune→A tuner for 87 instruments and 229 tunings, from guitar to bouzouki and timpani to kalimba, with reference tones and a drum tuner.Metronome→Sample-accurate click with odd meters, accents, swing, polyrhythms and setlists, with a swinging pendulum, conductor and more to watch. It listens while you play and tells you which beats you rush or drag.Beatpad→Nine drum pads to play with your keys, fingers or a MIDI pad: built-in kits (including a real acoustic kit), your own samples, a loop recorder and kits you can share.Restore→Digitize records and tapes side by side: record from your turntable or deck, split the tracks, fix clicks, hum, hiss and speed, fill in the release and export the album.Tag→Fill in everything distributors ask for (titles, credits, ISRC, UPC, cover art) without touching the audio, one song or a whole album, with a player so you can listen as you go.

Learn

Understand what you play, and get better at it.

Chords→Put notes on a guitar neck or piano and it names the chord, sharp or flat. Then build progressions with every move explained, so you learn why it works.Scales→47 scales on any neck or piano, which ones fit your chords, how the modes relate, and practice with the mic following along.Voice→A voice coach that listens: find your range, warm up, match pitches, hold steady notes, sing back tunes and find the right key for any song.
