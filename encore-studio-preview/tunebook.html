      What this is

      A tune book for folk, trad, polka, old-time and bluegrass players. Tunes are written in ABC, the plain-text music notation session players share. Type or paste ABC and the sheet music appears as you type. Press Play to hear it, slow it down, loop the hard part, and print a book for the next session or band night.

      ABC in one minute

      • The top lines are the header: T: title, R: tune type (reel, jig, polka...), M: time signature, L: the length of a plain note, K: key (always last).
      • Notes are letters. C D E F G A B are the low octave, c d e f g a b one higher. A comma goes lower (A,), an apostrophe higher (a').
      • Numbers make notes longer (A2), a slash shorter (A/). A>B is dotted. (3ABA is a triplet.
      • | is a bar line, |: and :| repeat a part, |1 and :|2 are first and second endings.
      • Chords go in quotes before a note: "G"GABc. Those are what the backing plays.

      Practising

      Pick Loop part A (or B) under Play, or click two notes in the music to loop just those bars. In Practice, turn on speed-up: it starts slow and gets a little faster every pass or two until it reaches your tempo. The notes light up as they play.

      Backing and click

      The backing plays the chord symbols in the tune. Oom-pah band is a tuba on the beat and an accordion on the off-beat, like a polka band; there's also guitar and bass, piano, and banjo and bass. Tunes with no chord symbols play without backing. Click adds a metronome on every beat, and Practice has a one-bar count-in.

      Sets

      A set is tunes played back to back, like at a session or a polka dance. Make one in Sets, put the tunes in order, choose how many times through, and play it.

      Keys and transposing

      The − and + by Key move the tune up or down a half step, chords too. "Show for" in Edit ABC writes it out for a B-flat clarinet or trumpet, an E-flat sax, or a horn. The ABC text stays as you typed it unless you press Keep this key.

      Your tunes

      Everything stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account. Clearing the browser's site data deletes your book, so download it as .abc now and then. The starter tunes are traditional and out of copyright.
