    Every sound has a short code with all its knobs in it. Copy it to share a sound or keep it in your notes, and paste one here to get the sound back.

    Quick start

    Pick a kind of sound at the top. Every click makes a new one. Like it? Add it to the pack. Almost? Mutate nudges it. Download a WAV, or the whole pack as a zip.

    The knobs

    Every sound is built from the same parts. Tone picks the waveform (square for 8-bit console style beeps, noise for explosions). Pitch moves the note: slides for jumps and lasers, arpeggio jumps for coins. Envelope shapes how it starts and fades. Filter makes it darker or thinner. Effects add flanger, crunch and lo-fi grit.

    Variations

    Games sound better when a repeated sound changes a little each time. "Make 4 variations" adds four close cousins of the sound to the pack, so your game can pick one at random.

    Which file settings?

    44,100 Hz, 16-bit works in every game engine. Use 48,000 Hz for video. 8-bit and 22,050 Hz make tiny files with a retro crunch. 32-bit float is best if you'll edit the sound further.
