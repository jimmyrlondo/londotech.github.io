Sections

A section can play many times in the song, and editing it changes every place it plays. "Copy this one" puts a copy in the selected spot of the song, so you can make a variation just there.

    A programmed part sounds like a machine when every hit is perfect. These make it feel played. They change the playback and every export the same way, every time.

    MIDI for your drum plugin

    The file has the drums on channel 10, every tempo and meter change, and a marker at each section. Drag it onto a track with EZdrummer, Superior Drummer, Addictive Drums (GM map), GetGood Drums or any General MIDI kit. If a drum plays the wrong sound, pick "My own map" and type the notes your plugin uses.

    Song file

    Save the song to keep a copy or move it to another computer. Open a saved song, or a MIDI drum file (from Guitar Pro, your DAW or a groove pack) to edit it here.

    The idea

    Write the drums for a whole song, section by section. Each section (intro, verse, chorus, breakdown) has its own groove, tempo and meter. Put sections in order on the strip to build the song. A section can play several times.

    Making a groove

    Pick a groove in the Grooves tab to start from: blast beats, gallops, double kick, d-beat, shuffles, odd meters. Then change it on the grid. The brush sets how hard a click hits. Ghost notes are the quiet snare taps between the big hits.

    Fills

    Set "Fill at the end" and the last beats of the section become a fill. "New fill" makes another one. Switch the grid to Fill to change it by hand. The next section starts with a crash.

    Feel

    Humanize moves hits a few milliseconds and varies how hard they land, like a real drummer. Fast parts get less movement so they stay tight. The same song always comes out the same way. "New take" rolls a different one.

    Tempo and odd meters

    Any meter works: 7/8, 5/4, 11/8, 12/8. Tempo is in quarter notes per minute. "Ramp to" speeds up or slows down across the section. All of it goes into the MIDI file, so your DAW follows.
