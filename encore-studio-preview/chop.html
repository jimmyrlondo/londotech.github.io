How to cut it

Sensitivity

Finds where each drum hit or note starts. More sensitivity finds quieter hits. Up to 16 slices, one per pad.

Splits the sample into even pieces. On a loop of whole bars, 16 parts are 16th notes. "Start at the first hit" skips any silence before the downbeat.

Click the waveform to add a cut. Drag a cut to move it. Double-click a cut (or right-click) to remove it.

Tempo

Load a sample and the tempo is found for you.

If the tempo is twice or half what you feel, use x2 or /2. Or tap along with the beat a few times.

Crackle is record surface noise. Tape adds a gentle wobble and warmth. Fewer bits and a lower sample rate add the grit of old samplers. Low-pass dulls the top. Pump ducks everything on each beat, like a sidechained kick.

The pattern

"Loops cleanly" folds the ringing tail of the last hits back onto the start, so the file repeats without a gap or a click. The lo-fi settings go in as you hear them.

The slices

One WAV per pad, with its pitch, reverse, volume and fades. Handy for loading into any sampler or drum machine.

Resample

Records the pattern, lo-fi and all, as a new sample and chops it again. A classic trick for layering. Undo brings the old one back.

What this is

A sampler for chopping. Load a short piece of audio (a drum loop, a few bars of a song you made, a field recording), cut it into up to 16 slices, and play the slices on pads. Then build a beat with the step sequencer, add lo-fi grit, and export it.

Quick start

  • Press "Try a demo loop", or load your own sample.
  • Hit the pads (click, tap, or your keyboard: Z X C V for the bottom row). Each pad plays one slice.
  • Pick a pad, then click squares in the step row to make it play on those 16th notes. Press Play.
  • Or press Record and play the pads in time: what you play is added to the pattern, snapped to the grid.
  • Swing pushes every second 16th later. A little (54 to 62%) gives the laid-back feel. 67% is a full triplet shuffle.

Cutting tips

  • "At each hit" is best for drums. "Equal parts" is best for loops of whole bars. "By hand" lets you put cuts exactly where you want.
  • If a slice clicks at the start or end, raise its Fade a little in the Pad tab.
  • Moving a cut switches to "By hand", so your changes are kept.
