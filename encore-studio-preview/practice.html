        What it's for

        Learning a song by ear. Slow the tricky part down without changing its pitch, loop it until your hands know it, then bring it back up to speed.

        Loops

        Drag across the waveform to make a loop, or press [ and ] while it plays to set the start and end. Drag an orange edge to fine-tune it. Loops are seamless: a tiny crossfade at the loop point stops it from clicking. Pick a rest between repeats if you need a moment to reset your hands.

        Get faster

        The trainer starts the loop slow and speeds it up a little every few repeats, until it reaches your goal. It's the classic way to get a fast lick clean.

        Speed and key

        ENCORE.'s own time-stretch changes the speed without changing the pitch, and the key without changing the speed. Use the key to match a song to your tuning or your voice, and fine tune to match records that are a little sharp or flat.

        Hearing the parts

        Vocal reduce takes out what's in the middle of the stereo mix (usually the lead vocal), but keeps the bass and kick. The focus buttons lift one part of the sound: bass, vocals, or guitar and keys. Results depend on how the song was mixed.
