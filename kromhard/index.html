About Kromhard

Built on relationships, since 1958

Kromhard Twist Drill Co. was founded in 1958 by James H. Griffin. Jim earned a bachelor's degree in industrial engineering and a master's degree in business administration to prepare for a career in tool sales - then carried an attaché case of drills business to business across Akron to build the company's first customers.

Nearly seven decades later, Kromhard is one of the nation's largest suppliers of in-stock, quality industrial tools and supplies, still serving the same tool-using trades Jim set out to win over: machine shops, fabricators, maintenance departments, and contractors who need the right part in stock, today.

This concept keeps that same in-stock, relationship-driven model - it just gives customers a real search bar and a cart instead of a phone call being the only way in.
