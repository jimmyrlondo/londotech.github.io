Rescue old recordings.

For transfers of LPs and 45s, 78s, cassettes, 8-tracks, reel-to-reel and CDs. Restore listens first and tells you, in plain English, what it hears: clicks, hum, hiss, a deck running fast, wow, dropouts. Then it fixes only that, gently, with before and after a click apart. Your original file is never changed.
