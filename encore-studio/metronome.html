Time check

Play along and it listens: are you rushing or dragging, on which beats, how steady, and do you hold the tempo when the click goes quiet?

1. Calibrate (once for each setup)

Not calibrated yet
Use speakers, not headphones, for this step, with the mic where it will be when you play. It plays 12 clicks and listens for them through the mic, so stay quiet. This measures your device's own delay so "early" and "late" are real. Do it again if you change the input, the output or the browser.
No speakers the mic can hear? Calibrate by clapping: clap exactly with 16 clicks (it uses the last 12).

2. Play

Wear headphones now if you can: then the mic only hears you, which is ideal. Wired ones, since Bluetooth adds a delay of its own. If the mic hears the click, your notes look too perfect, and it will tell you.

Find the BPM

--BPM
Tap along with the song on the beat. After 4 taps you'll see a tempo, and it gets more exact the longer you tap.
Listening works best on music with a clear beat. If the number looks half or double what you feel, use ÷2 or ×2. Song files stay on your device.
