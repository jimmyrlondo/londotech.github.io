        Get it printed

        PNGs are 300 dpi with 1/8 inch bleed on every edge, the way printers and duplicators want them. Print the outside, then the inside on the back of the same sheet (flip on the long edge). Keep words inside the dotted safe area.

        What this is for

        Planning a cassette release: which songs go on side A and side B, whether they fit the tape, how the tape will sound, the audio files a duplicator needs, and the printed J-card that folds into the case.

        Planning the sides

        • Add songs. Each one goes on side A until it's full, then side B. Its length is read from the file.
        • Drag songs between the sides and up or down (or focus a song and use the arrow keys). The bars show how full each side is.
        • A tape number is minutes for both sides: a C60 holds 30 minutes a side. Leave about 15 seconds spare at the end of each side, because tapes vary a little.
        • Try to keep the sides within a minute or two of each other, so nobody fast-forwards through a long stretch of blank tape. The tips under the sides suggest a move when they're uneven.

        Tape sound

        Tape age is one slider for the whole character. The fine controls let you set each part. What you hear while playing is the same tape sound that goes into a tape-style export.

        Exports

        • For a duplicator: send one clean WAV per side (16-bit 44.1 kHz is the safest choice) and the tracklist. Don't add the tape sound: the real tape adds its own. Some duplicators like a few seconds of silence at the start of each side; check what yours asks for.
        • Tape-style release: the same sides with the tape sound printed in, ready to upload.
        • Leave a little headroom in your masters (peaks around -1 dB). Very squashed masters can distort on tape.

        The J-card

        The card is the standard size: back flap 1 1/16 in, spine 1/2 in, front 2 9/16 in, 4 in tall, with 1/8 in bleed. The outside has the back flap, spine and front; the inside holds the track list and your notes. Print at 100% (actual size), never "fit to page".
