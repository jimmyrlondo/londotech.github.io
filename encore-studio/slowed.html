    What it does

    Makes the edits you hear all over short videos: slowed down with a big dreamy reverb, or sped up and brighter. Pick a preset, tweak it while it plays, then export the whole song.

    Vinyl style or keep the key

    Normally the pitch follows the speed, like playing a record slower or faster. That's the classic slowed and nightcore sound. Tick "Keep the key" to change only the speed: ENCORE.'s own time-stretch keeps every note where it was.

    The reverb

    The rooms are made from scratch on your device: highs fade faster than lows, like a real space. "Keep the low end clean" stops the bass and kick from turning into rumble inside the reverb, the most common reason these edits sound muddy.

    Exporting

    The export runs the same sound engine as the preview, so the file sounds like what you heard. A gentle limiter keeps it from clipping, and the reverb is allowed to ring out at the end instead of being cut off.
