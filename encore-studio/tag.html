    Tag your tracks.

    Drop one song or a whole album. See every tag already in the files, fill in titles, credits, cover art, lyrics, ISRCs and anything else, then save new copies.

    Tags are written straight into the file without re-encoding: the audio stays bit for bit the same, and every saved copy is checked to prove it.

    What distributors (DistroKid, CD Baby, TuneCore and the like) ask for when you send a song in. Everything else is in More fields.
