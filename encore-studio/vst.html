Installing, updating and removing

First run: the installer isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway.
Then: open your DAW and scan for new plugins. In Cubase: Studio > VST Plug-in Manager, then Rescan.
Change your mind: run the installer again, or use Modify on ENCORE. VST in Windows Settings > Apps, to add or remove any plugin. Uninstall there removes them all. Your presets in Documents\Encore are never touched.

What's inside

VOX VOCALS

Your whole vocal chain in one plugin, in the order you'd build it by hand.

  • Clean up: low cut, gate and de-ess
  • Tune and harmony
  • A rider that evens out the level, then tone, dynamics and space

VERB REVERB

Spaces from a small room to something enormous.

  • Room, Chamber, Plate, Hall, Huge (with shimmer) and Spring
  • Ducking, so the reverb steps back while you sing or play
  • Movement and width

EQUALIZE EQ

An 8-band EQ you shape by dragging, over a live spectrum.

  • Bells, shelves and cuts up to 48 dB per octave
  • Smart: Carve ducks one track's EQ when another plays, for a kick and bass or a vocal and guitars
  • Smart bands find resonances and only cut when they ring

MASTER MASTERING

The same mastering engine as ENCORE. Master on the web, on your mix bus.

  • 8 styles; Listen learns your song's level and tone
  • A true-peak limiter and loudness targets for streaming
  • A loudness-matched A/B, so louder never fools you

STRUM ACOUSTIC GUITAR

The usual acoustic guitar problems, each with its own fix.

  • Body: make a pickup sound miked, learned from your own mic or a general curve
  • Boom, Squeak and Pick tame what close mics and strings add
  • Double for a wide strummed part, and a loudness-matched A/B
Free. The same plugins are built into ENCORE. Basement Studio.
