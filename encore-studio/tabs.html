        What this is
        
        
        A tab maker for kalimba, handpan and steel tongue drum. Write a tune by clicking the instrument, typing, playing a MIDI keyboard or playing into the mic, and it's shown three ways at once: number tab, letter names and the instrument with the notes lit up.
        
        
        Reading the tab
        
        
        Kalimba and tongue drum: 1 to 7 are the notes of the instrument's scale (on a C kalimba, 1 is C). A dot above means one octave up, a dot below one octave down. Numbers stacked on top of each other are played together.
        
        
        Handpan: D is the ding in the middle, then 1, 2, 3 are the fields from the lowest note up. They zigzag around the pan, just like on the drawing.
        
        
        Rhythm: a plain number is one beat. A line under it halves it (two lines, a quarter). Each dash after it holds one more beat. A dot after it makes it one and a half times as long. 0 is a rest.
        
        
        Writing
        
        
        Pick a length, then click notes. Click a note in the tab to select it: the next note you play replaces it, and the length buttons change it. Hold Shift to stack a chord. "Record in time" counts you in and writes what you play with its rhythm.
        
        
        Converting
        
        
        Tabs for a 17-key kalimba don't fit an 8-key, and a song in C won't sit on a D Kurd handpan. Convert tries every key and picks the one where most notes land. Notes that still don't fit are moved an octave if the instrument has that letter somewhere, or marked with a red ? so you can rewrite them.
