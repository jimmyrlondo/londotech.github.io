ENCORE: GREEN ROOM

Everything you need before you go on.

Free tools for musicians: tune up, warm up, practice, write, record a show, master it, rescue an old record and get your release ready. Pick one and go.

Everything stays on your device. Nothing you record, open or make is uploaded. The tools run right here in your browser.

Make and release

MasterMaster a finished mix: pick a sound, check your mix, compare with a song you love, hear it the way Spotify and Apple will play it, and export tagged, release-ready files. LiveRecord a show or drop in the recording. It finds the songs, masters them all evenly, and gets the live album ready to sell. Last CallThe check before you release: loudness on every streaming service, clipping, mono, clicks at the start and end, format, tags and cover art, with plain fixes. TagFill in everything distributors ask for (titles, credits, ISRC, UPC, cover art) without touching the audio, one song or a whole album, with a player so you can listen as you go. ConvertDrop a file, get an MP3 (or WAV, FLAC, AAC, Opus) with its tags and cover kept. One song or a whole folder.

Play, practice and learn

TuneA tuner for 87 instruments and 229 tunings, from guitar to bouzouki and timpani to kalimba, with reference tones and a drum tuner. MetronomeSample-accurate click with odd meters, accents, swing, polyrhythms and setlists. It listens while you play and tells you which beats you rush or drag. Finds a song's BPM too. BeatpadNine drum pads to play with your keys, fingers or a MIDI pad: built-in kits (including a real acoustic kit), your own samples, a loop recorder and kits you can share. VoiceA voice coach that listens: find your range, warm up, match pitches, hold steady notes, sing back tunes and find the right key for any song. ChordsPut notes on a guitar neck or piano and it names the chord. Then build progressions with every move explained, so you learn why it works. Scales47 scales on any neck or piano, which ones fit your chords, how the modes relate, and practice with the mic following along.

Rescue and record

RestoreDigitize records and tapes side by side: record from your turntable or deck, split the tracks, fix clicks, hum, hiss and speed, fill in the release and export the album. Basement Studio DESKTOP APPEncore's free, open-source studio for your computer: record the whole band, edit, mix with Encore's plugins built in, and send finished songs to Encore.
