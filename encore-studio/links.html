Works with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, Deezer, TIDAL, Amazon, SoundCloud and Bandcamp links. We look the link up through song.link and Apple's free music catalogue, then fill in what they find. You can change anything after.

song.link key (optional)

song.link finds every streaming service from one link, but it now asks for a free key. To get one, email developers@song.link and paste it here. Without it, we still find Apple Music and the details from your link, and you add the rest by hand. The key stays in this browser and never goes in your page.

Where do I get pre-save links?
  1. DistroKid: open your release and copy its HyperFollow link. One link covers Spotify, Apple Music and more, so add it as a single button.
  2. TuneCore, CD Baby, Amuse and others: look for a pre-save or smart link on your release's page.
  3. Apple Music pre-add: once your distributor sets up a pre-order, the Apple Music album page is your pre-add link.

Your form's code
Where do I find this in Kit?
  1. In Kit, go to Grow, then Landing Pages & Forms, and open your form (or make a simple one).
  2. Click Publish and choose HTML (not JavaScript).
  3. Copy the code and paste it above.
Where do I find this in Mailchimp?
  1. In Mailchimp, go to Audience, then Signup forms, and pick Embedded form.
  2. Click Continue, then Copy Code.
  3. Paste it above.

When a fan signs up, Kit or Mailchimp opens in a new tab to confirm it.

2. Where it lives

After you put the page online, paste its address here. It makes your QR code and gives link previews the right address. Then download the page again.

3. QR code

PNG for posts and stories, SVG for posters and merch (it stays sharp at any size). Test it with your phone camera before you print.

4. Where to put it (free)
  1. Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop, drag your page in (put it in a folder, rename it index.html for a clean address). Make a free account to keep it online.
  2. GitHub Pages: make a free repository, upload the file, then turn on Pages in Settings.
  3. Your own website: upload the file like any other page, or ask whoever runs your site.
